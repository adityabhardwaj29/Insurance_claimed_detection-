# Consolidated Final Research Results

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Graph-Enhanced Insurance Claim Fraud Detection Platform** successfully integrates tabular feature engineering, deterministic text similarity, supervised gradient boosting, unsupervised anomaly detection, and heterogeneous knowledge graph analysis into a single unified decision-support platform.

Across the entire dataset of 320 claims:
- **Ground-Truth Fraud Prevalence**: 16.25% (52 fraud claims, 268 legitimate claims).
- **Hybrid Risk Distribution**:
  - **CRITICAL Risk** ($\ge 0.75$): Captured high-density fraud candidates with multiple compounding signals.
  - **HIGH Risk** ($[0.55, 0.75)$): Priority investigation queue for SIU review.
  - **MEDIUM Risk** ($[0.30, 0.55)$): Monitored or secondary review claims.
  - **LOW Risk** ($< 0.30$): Fast-track processing claims.
- **Explainability**: 100% of high-risk claims receive both SHAP local feature attributions and topological graph evidence.
